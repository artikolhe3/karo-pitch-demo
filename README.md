# Karo Pitch – Startup Pitch Platform (Demo Landing Page)

## Project Overview

This project is a **demo landing page for Karo Pitch**, a startup pitch platform by KaroStartup.

Karo Pitch is designed to help **early-stage founders from across India**, especially from **Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities**, pitch their businesses directly to investors.

The goal of this project was to design a **modern, clean, and investor-focused homepage** that clearly explains the Karo Pitch platform and encourages startups to apply.

## Features

The landing page includes the following sections:

### 1. Hero Section
Headline:  
**"Pitch Your Startup to India’s Top Investors."**

Includes call-to-action buttons:
- Apply to Pitch
- Explore Startups

---

### 2. About Karo Pitch

Explains the mission of Karo Pitch to connect founders with investors and support entrepreneurs across Bharat.

---

### 3. How It Works

A simple 4-step startup pitching process:

1. Apply with your pitch deck  
2. Get shortlisted by KaroStartup  
3. Pitch live to investors  
4. Raise funding and scale

---

### 4. Who Can Apply

Startup categories supported:

- D2C Brands
- Consumer Startups
- MSMEs
- SaaS Startups
- Manufacturing Businesses
- Bharat-focused Startups

---

### 5. Investors Section

A section highlighting that investors will participate in pitch events and discover promising startups.

---

### 6. Featured Startups

Example startup cards displaying:

- Startup Name
- Category
- Short Description

---

### 7. Investor Dashboard (Demo)

Shows sample platform metrics such as:

- Total Startups
- Live Pitches
- Funding Raised

---

### 8. Startup Application Form

A form where founders can submit:

- Founder Name
- Email
- Startup Name
- Category
- Pitch Deck Upload

---

### 9. About KaroStartup

Information about KaroStartup as one of India's startup storytelling platforms with a large founder community.

---

### 10. Final Call to Action

Encourages founders to apply or partner with the platform.
